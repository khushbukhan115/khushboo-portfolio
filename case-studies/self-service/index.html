Case study · Self-Service Portal

From sales-gated to self-serve.

Shufti is an identity-verification platform that, for most of its history, sold only through sales calls. I redesigned the commercial surface so customers could discover, price, sign up, and verify on their own, turning a sales bottleneck into a revenue engine.

The problem

A funnel that could not self-serve.

The portal existed. It just did not serve. It asked you to commit as a company, declare your country, and clear a five-field form before you saw a single screen of the product.

The leadership concern was real: many believed enterprise buyers would never purchase a compliance platform without talking to sales. The job was to prove they would, while reducing friction and raising paid conversion.

First, a clean cohort
Roughly 31% of the raw records were synthetic QA accounts and about 18% more were disposable-email signups; cleaned, the real cohort was around 275. On that base, Free Forever failed two ways at once: nearly half of non-converters never activated, and nearly half stayed a month or more without ever paying. Activation and monetization were separate problems.
You are not designing a product. You are designing a revenue funnel with a UI attached.
The reframe, and the move

Design the funnel, not the page.

The data reframed the problem. Post-signup, users showed strong buying intent, but on Vendor Comparison and Integrations, not on Plans and Billing, the page built to convert it. So I treated pricing, signup, onboarding, and lifecycle as one system with one goal: qualified, activated revenue.

The tier the old pricing implied for the middle was, in the data, a dead tier: seven users in thirteen months, and exactly one person who ever walked the full Free to Essentials to Enterprise ladder. So I rebuilt the middle rather than decorating it. The keystone move was Essentials Plus, a modular, volume-based tier. Users configure only the checks they need on a slider, pay a transparent per-verification price through Stripe, and add services later with one change and no sales call. That single product closes the mid-market gap and breaks the $2,500 clustering into a real spectrum, alongside a one-click signup, a pricing calculator, and same-day first value.
