## DESCRIPTION (paste, ~1,180 characters, within Toptal's 200 to 1,250)
• Turned an enterprise-only gift-card platform into a self-serve product any business can run, from a café to a global brand
• Took merchants from sign-up to a live, sellable branded gift card with no engineering and no setup cost
• Designed a guided card creator with a live preview, built around three decisions: brand, value, and message
• Kept the terms of the deal (value, validity, redemption) clear for the buyer, the recipient, and the merchant
• Shipped in 2022, pay-as-you-go, launched with talabat, and still live today

As Lead UX/UI Designer I owned the merchant experience end to end: onboarding, the workspace, the card creator, the branded storefront, and the recipient and redemption views. Merit Incentives already ran enterprise gift-card infrastructure (issuance, payments, redemption, a large merchant network) but delivered it only as custom, high-touch programmes, leaving most small businesses unable to offer gifting at all. The design idea was subtraction: decide the fewest decisions a merchant must make to create a trustworthy, sellable card, and let the platform carry the rest. It turned a capability only enterprises could commission into a product a non-specialist can run alone.
